# Third-party notices

This fork starts from `lukechampine.com/blake3` v1.4.1, Copyright 2020 Luke
Champine, under the MIT license in `LICENSE`.

The ARM64 NEON kernels in `guts/compress_arm64.s`, their dispatch integration
in `guts/compress_arm64.go`, and the bounded scheduling design in `parallel.go`
and `guts/parallel.go` were ported or adapted from
`goforge.dev/blake3sum` v1.0.0, Copyright 2026 brain-fuel, under the MIT license
in `LICENSE-GOFORGE`.

The AMD64 kernels in `guts/compress_amd64.s` are generated by `avo/gen.go`,
which extends upstream's generator. Two of its techniques come from the BLAKE3
reference implementation (https://github.com/BLAKE3-team/BLAKE3, released
under CC0 1.0 or Apache License 2.0 at the user's option): transposing input
blocks with loads that combine two inputs, and the shuffle sequence that
permutes the message words between rounds of a single-block compression.
